## 🔬 Featured Research & Projects

### [PD-aware Fine-tuning](https://github.com/nicoyoung1101/pd-aware-finetuning)
Preserving clinically relevant Parkinsonian gait features by adapting **0.48% of TransPose's parameters**, with six or head-free five virtual IMUs. Includes a subject-wise evaluation protocol, reproducible analysis, and SMPL mesh motion demonstrations.

[![SMPL gait reconstruction: ground truth and fine-tuned models](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nicoyoung1101/pd-aware-finetuning/main/assets/gait-reconstruction.gif)](https://github.com/nicoyoung1101/pd-aware-finetuning)

**ICNR 2026 · Poster P-113** · [Project page](https://nicoyoung1101.github.io/pd-gait-imu/) · [Code & results](https://github.com/nicoyoung1101/pd-aware-finetuning)

### [PD Gait Benchmark](https://github.com/nicoyoung1101/pd-gait-benchmark)
A five-model benchmark examining **pose accuracy, gait-feature preservation, and clinical task utility** on MoCap-derived virtual IMUs. The benchmark motivates the fine-tuning study and includes evaluation code, aggregate results, and SMPL visual comparisons.

*An Assessment of Gait Feature Preservation in Sparse Inertial Pose Estimation for Parkinsonian Gait Analysis*  
**BHI 2026 · Accepted** · **TransPose · PIP raw · DynaIP · PNP · TIP** · [Code & results](https://github.com/nicoyoung1101/pd-gait-benchmark)
